🏛️ Federal Law — MOHRE (UAE Labour Law No. 33 of 2021)

  • Applies to all mainland companies in UAE across all Emirates
  • Mandatory WPS (Wage Protection System) — salaries must be paid within 10 days of due date
  • Maximum working hours: 8 hours/day or 48 hours/week (36 hrs in Ramadan)
  • Annual leave: 30 calendar days after 1 year; 2 days/month in first year
  • Overtime: 125% of normal rate; 150% for Fridays and public holidays
  • Gratuity: 21 days basic/year (first 5 yrs) + 30 days basic/year (beyond 5 yrs). Capped at 2 years' salary
  • Notice period: minimum 30 days (less than 5 yrs); 60 days (5+ yrs)
  • Health insurance mandatory (varies by Emirate — DHA/HAAD/MOH)
  • Article 44 dismissal (without notice): theft, assault, policy violations

⚓ JAFZA — Jebel Ali Free Zone Authority

  • JAFZA companies follow UAE Federal Labour Law with additional JAFZA Employment Regulations
  • JAFZA Employment Regulations 2011 govern employment in Jebel Ali Free Zone
  • Gratuity is calculated same as MOHRE: 21 days for first 5 years, 30 days thereafter
  • Annual leave: 30 calendar days per year (after 1 year)
  • Working hours: 48 hours/week standard; may differ per company policy within JAFZA
  • End of service settlement must be paid within 14 days of termination
  • Medical insurance mandatory for all employees
  • JAFZA-issued employment visas subject to GDRFA (Dubai) requirements

🏢 Free Zone Employment (FZE) — DMCC, DAFZA, RAKEZ, etc.

  • FZE companies operate under their respective Free Zone Authority regulations
  • Generally follow UAE Federal Labour Law (Law 33/2021) for employment standards
  • DMCC (Dubai Multi Commodities Centre) — follows Dubai Labour Law with DMCC overlay
  • DAFZA (Dubai Airport Free Zone) — aligned with MOHRE federal law
  • RAKEZ (Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone) — RAK Labour regulations apply
  • Gratuity calculation same as Federal: 21 days (1–5 yrs) + 30 days (5+ yrs)
  • Health insurance: mandatory per Emirate health authority regulations

🏙️ DIFC — Dubai International Financial Centre

  • Governed by DIFC Employment Law No. 4 of 2021 — independent of Federal Labour Law
  • End of Service scheme replaced with mandatory DEWS (DIFC Employee Workplace Savings)
  • DEWS: Employer contributes 5.83% (less than 5 yrs) or 8.33% (5+ yrs) of monthly salary monthly
  • Annual leave: 20 working days minimum (DIFC standard)
  • Working hours: 48 hours/week maximum
  • Maternity leave: 65 working days; Paternity leave: 5 working days
  • Notice period: 30 days minimum; up to 90 days for senior roles

🌊 ADGM — Abu Dhabi Global Market

  • Governed by ADGM Employment Regulations 2019 — independent legal framework
  • End of Service Gratuity aligned with Federal Law (21 days / 30 days) but calculated on total salary
  • Annual leave: 25 working days per year
  • Working hours: 45 hours/week standard
  • Sick leave: 10 days full pay + 20 days half pay + 30 days unpaid
  • Maternity leave: 65 calendar days (ADGM)
  • ADGM Court has exclusive jurisdiction over employment disputes within ADGM
